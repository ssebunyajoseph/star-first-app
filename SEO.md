# SEO & Social Media Discovery Guide

Your Star first App is now fully optimized to be discovered on Google, social media, and other platforms!

## 🔍 How It Works

### Google Search
When someone searches for "todo app", "task manager", or "free offline app":

1. **Google crawls your site** using the `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`
2. **Structured data** (JSON-LD) tells Google exactly what your app is
3. **Meta tags** provide descriptions and keywords
4. **Rich snippets** appear in search results with:
   - App name and description
   - Installation button
   - Rating and reviews
   - Screenshot thumbnails

### Social Media Preview
When someone shares your app link on:

**Facebook / WhatsApp:**

**LinkedIn:**
